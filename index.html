CIFAKE – Deep Learning-Based Detection of Real and AI-Generated Synthetic Images

Conducted an experimental investigation of deep-learning approaches for distinguishing real images from AI-generated synthetic images, focusing on model comparison, robustness, and generalization.

97.21% Best CIFAKE Test Accuracy (Swin-Tiny · F1: 97.23%)
120,000 Images Evaluated (100k Train / 20k Test · 32×32 px)
60.13% Held-Out Balanced Accuracy (+10.20 pp from 49.93%)

CIFAKE Test Set Model Comparison

Model CIFAKE Test Accuracy F1-score
ResNet-18 95.64% 95.52%
Vision Transformer Tiny (ViT-Tiny) 96.76% 96.68%
Swin Transformer Tiny (Swin-Tiny) 97.21% 97.23%
  • Dataset Protocol: Evaluated across 120,000 images at 32 × 32 pixel resolution — 100,000 training images (50,000 real, 50,000 synthetic) and 20,000 testing images (10,000 real, 10,000 synthetic).
  • Robustness & Distribution Shift: Tested robustness under increasing Gaussian noise and evaluated generalization on an external dataset and held-out generator data, identifying significant performance degradation under distribution shifts.
  • Generalization Experiment: Investigated generator-diverse training combined with JPEG augmentation, improving held-out balanced accuracy from 49.93% to 60.13% (a 10.20 percentage-point improvement).
